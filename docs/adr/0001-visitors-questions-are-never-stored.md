# Visitors' questions are never stored on the Kit Mac

At a Community Point, strangers ask the AI about injuries, illness and their situation. A conversation lives
only in the visitor's own browser: the Kit Mac does not save, log or count questions or answers, and the AI
search sends them to no app that keeps chat history. Any future feature (logs, statistics, a Keeper view) must
keep it that way, because a Kit Mac can be lost, seized or used by the next person.
