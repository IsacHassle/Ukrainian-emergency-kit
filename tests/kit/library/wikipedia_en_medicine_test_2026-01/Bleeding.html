Bleeding

Bleeding is the loss of blood from the body, inside or outside. Heavy bleeding can lead to shock and death within minutes if it is not controlled.

Treatment

Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth and keep pressing. Raise the injured limb if you can. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top and do not remove the first layer.

Prevention

Keep sharp tools stored safely and wear protective gear when working with them in the garden or at home.
