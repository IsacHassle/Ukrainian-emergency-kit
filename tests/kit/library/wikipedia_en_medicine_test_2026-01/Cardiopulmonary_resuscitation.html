Cardiopulmonary resuscitation

Cardiopulmonary resuscitation is an emergency procedure used when someone's heart or breathing has stopped. It combines chest compressions with rescue breaths.

First aid

Push hard and fast in the centre of the chest, about twice per second, and keep going until help arrives or the person starts breathing again. Do not stop to check too often.

Prevention

Learning the technique in a course makes it easier to act calmly when it matters most for someone you love.
