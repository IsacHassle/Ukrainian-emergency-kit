Tourniquet

A tourniquet is a device that applies pressure to a limb to stop severe bleeding. It is used in first aid when direct pressure on a wound is not enough to control the blood loss.

First aid

Place the tourniquet two to three fingers above the wound, never over a joint. Tighten until the bleeding stops, note the time, and do not loosen it. Get the injured person to medical care as fast as possible.

Complications

A tourniquet left on for hours can damage nerves and muscle, but a life is worth more than a limb in severe bleeding.
